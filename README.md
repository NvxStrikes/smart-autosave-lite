## What It Does

Smart AutoSave+ Lite silently saves a backup copy of your Blender project on a timer you control. No popups, no interruptions — just quiet protection running in the background.

Every Blender artist has been here:
> *"It's 2 AM. I've been working for 3 hours. Blender just crashed."*

This addon makes sure that never costs you your work.

---

## Features

- ✅ Auto-save on a custom interval (1–60 minutes)
- ✅ Force a backup instantly with one click
- ✅ Keeps your last 3 backups automatically
- ✅ Restore any recent backup straight from the panel
- ✅ Open your backup folder directly from Blender
- ✅ Backups are separate copies — your original `.blend` is never overwritten
- ✅ Windows, macOS & Linux
- ✅ Tested on Blender 4.2 and 5.0
- ✅ Free & open source (GPL v3)

## Installation

1. Download **`Autosave_Lite.zip`** from this repo (don't unzip it)
2. In Blender: **Edit → Preferences → Add-ons**
3. Click the **▾** dropdown (top right) → **Install from Disk…** and select the zip
4. Enable **Smart AutoSave+ Lite** in the list
5. Open the **N-Panel** (press `N` in the 3D viewport) → **Smart AutoSave+ Lite** tab

---

## Usage

1. Save your `.blend` file first (`Ctrl+S`). Lite skips unsaved files.
2. Set your preferred interval in the panel
3. That's it — backups go to an `autosaves/` folder next to your `.blend`, named `YourFile_Lite_Backup_N.blend`
